What this is

The Historiotheque is my art operation: the living archive, laboratory, and publishing house for everything I make and study. I work across images, sounds, and texts — painting, experimental music and sound design, and novelistic, essayistic, and theoretical writing.

Everything here is documented in the open — the work, the process, and the thinking behind it — under version control, so the record stays intact and traceable. This site is the front door. The work itself lives in the repositories linked below.
