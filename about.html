About

I am Alex Gagnon. I sign my work A.G.

I am an interdisciplinary artist-researcher. My practice moves across images, sounds, and texts — painting (analog and digital), experimental music and sound design, and novelistic, essayistic, and theoretical writing.

The Historiotheque is my art operation: the living archive, laboratory, and publishing house for everything I make and study. I am its sole operator. Every project here is documented in the open — the work, the process, and the thinking behind it — under version control, so the record stays intact and traceable.

I work alone, as an independent researcher, and I publish the way the sciences do: with persistent identifiers, so anything I claim can be found, cited, and checked.
